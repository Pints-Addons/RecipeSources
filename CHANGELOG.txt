Pints Recipe Sources - Changelog

v3.2.1
- WoW Forever: recipes discovered while crafting are noted (what you were crafting and your skill) for all your
  characters, and listed under "Discover while crafting".

v3.2
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- The Learnable list attaches to the modern professions window and reads its recipes.
- Separate position settings for the modern window, lined up by default.

v3.1
NEW
- "Other faction" option: tick the box at the top of the Learnable list to also see
  recipes sold by the other faction's vendors or given by their quests. They're
  tagged [Horde] or [Alliance] in red and listed after your own faction's sources.
  These recipes are items, so a friend on the other side or the neutral auction house
  can get them to you. Other-faction trainers stay hidden, since they can't teach you.
  Off by default. (Thanks SawyerMBlack for the suggestion!)

NOTES
- Adds 16 recipes for Alliance and 41 for Horde that were hidden before, and extra
  sources to over 100 more.
- Your setting is saved account-wide.
