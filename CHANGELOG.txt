Pints Recipe Sources - Changelog

v3.2.8
- WoW Forever: Tailoring's new recipes are in Learnable Recipes - 168 Forever patterns Classic doesn't have (Stormsewn,
  Pristine/Shadow/Silky and the other Filigreed sets, Frothing and Nether- sets, Venomspew and Swarmtender, Manaflare,
  Conviction and Demonheart, reagent bags, Faction Banner, Spinning Wheel, Loom and more), each with its trainer, vendor
  (and zone - Merchant's Favor ones too), drop or quest, what it makes and its cost. From Wowhead's WoW Forever database.
- WoW Forever: the same for Alchemy (67 recipes), Engineering (57), Blacksmithing (191) and Leatherworking (250) - Forever's
  own recipes Classic doesn't have, each with its trainer, vendor and zone (Merchant's Favor and other non-gold costs
  named), drop or quest, what it makes and its cost.

v3.2.7
- Learnable Recipes learns who sells and teaches what, and where: open a vendor or trainer and its recipes show that
  vendor or trainer with their real place (marked "seen") - WoW Forever moved and added some. Limited stock too.

v3.2.6
- Learnable Recipes: a rank you already have (Journeyman, Expert, Artisan) no longer shows as learnable - your skill cap
  says which you have even when the game doesn't list the rank itself as known.

v3.2.5
- WoW Forever: the Learnable Recipes panel sits to the right of the profession window's tabs instead of over them.

v3.2.4
- WoW Forever: comes with trainer prices seen in Forever, so recipes show a real cost before you've visited the trainer.
- Trainer visits now also note each rank's price and level (to fill in more prices in later updates).

v3.2.3
- WoW Forever: no more errors when the game hides a system chat message from add-ons.

v3.2.2
- WoW Forever: money shows with the gold, silver and copper icons (the game's own coin text showed bare digits).

v3.2.1
- WoW Forever: recipes discovered while crafting are noted (what you were crafting and your skill) for all your
  characters, and listed under "Discover while crafting".

v3.2
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- The Learnable list attaches to the modern professions window and reads its recipes.
- Separate position settings for the modern window, lined up by default.

v3.1
NEW
- "Other faction" option: tick the box at the top of the Learnable list to also see
  recipes sold by the other faction's vendors or given by their quests. They're
  tagged [Horde] or [Alliance] in red and listed after your own faction's sources.
  These recipes are items, so a friend on the other side or the neutral auction house
  can get them to you. Other-faction trainers stay hidden, since they can't teach you.
  Off by default. (Thanks SawyerMBlack for the suggestion!)

NOTES
- Adds 16 recipes for Alliance and 41 for Horde that were hidden before, and extra
  sources to over 100 more.
- Your setting is saved account-wide.
